Game UX Heuristic Audit

Use this tool to run your self-audit. Rate every checkpoint against your own screens, one category at a time.

  1. Open a category. Work through every checkpoint, even the ones you feel sure about.
  2. Open your Figma screens and rate how well each one meets the checkpoint.
  3. For anything Weak or Missing, name the exact screen and element, pick a severity, and write what breaks.
  4. In the Plan tab, pick your top 5 to 6 fixes. Then copy or download the report.

Answers save in this browser only. Download the PDF before you switch devices or clear browser data.
